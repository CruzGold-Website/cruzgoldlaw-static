FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Immigration Law Explained

What happens if I get a DUI in New Jersey for the first time?

A first-time DUI in New Jersey can result in license suspension, fines, mandatory classes, insurance surcharges, and possible jail time, depending on your BAC level. However, an experienced attorney may be able to reduce or dismiss the charges depending on the facts of your case.

Can I fight a traffic ticket in New Jersey, or should I just pay it?

You can absolutely fight a traffic ticket, and in many cases, it’s worth doing. Pleading guilty and paying a fine may result in points on your license, higher insurance rates, and even license suspension.

Will I lose my license if I refuse a breathalyzer test in New Jersey?

Yes, refusing a breathalyzer in New Jersey triggers automatic license suspension under the state’s implied consent law. However, you may still have defenses, and we can challenge the validity of the stop, the request, or the refusal itself.
