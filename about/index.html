About Cruz Gold & Associates

A family firm, since 1995.

At Cruz Gold & Associates, we believe that strong legal representation begins with genuine care for our clients. Our mission is to blend legal knowledge with understanding, ensuring that every individual who comes to us feels heard and supported. Founded by Marta Cruz Gold and led by Zachary Gold, whose family’s immigration story fuels his mission, we are committed to helping others build their future through experienced, empathetic guidance. Our firm specializes in immigration law, always putting our clients’ needs at the forefront of everything we do.

Our story

From a Mercer County practice to four offices

  1. 1995The practice opens

    After law school at Rutgers, Marta Cruz Gold opens The Law Office of Marta Cruz Gold and becomes a community leader in Mercer County.

  2. ThenThe next generation

    Zachary Gold starts his career as a paralegal and earns his Juris Doctor at Drexel University.

  3. 2023Marta’s legacy

    Marta passes away on June 12, 2023. Cruz Gold & Associates carries on her mission of compassionate legal services.

  4. TodayLed by Zachary Gold

Owner and attorney

Zachary Gold, Esq.

With over 7 years of legal experience, Zachary Gold leads Cruz Gold & Associates as owner and lead attorney. Starting as a paralegal, he now specializes in immigration law matters. A Rutgers University alumnus, Zachary earned his Juris Doctor from Drexel University. His passion for the law is rooted in personal history inspired by his parents’ immigration to the U.S.; he is driven by the quest to create opportunities for others, just as his parents sought for their family.

At Cruz Gold & Associates, Zachary’s straightforward approach emphasizes genuine client care. He stays ahead of legal trends and continuously expands his knowledge through seminars and conferences to better serve his clients. He specializes in immigration law.

As a member of the American Immigration Lawyers Association, Zachary combines expertise with personal insight into the immigration process. He ensures client privacy with rigorous confidentiality practices and encrypted systems. Outside the office, he enjoys fitness, ice hockey, Formula 1, and family time, maintaining a balance between a healthy body and a sharp mind.
