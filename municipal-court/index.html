Municipal Court

At Cruz Gold & Associates, we provide experienced legal support for individuals facing municipal court matters in New Jersey. Whether it’s a traffic violation or DUI charge, we are committed to protecting your rights and achieving the best possible outcome for your case.

OUR FOCUS

Protecting Your Rights In Court

Municipal court cases can be complex and carry serious consequences, impacting your driving privileges, fines, or even your freedom. At Cruz Gold & Associates, we offer thorough representation for a variety of municipal court matters, including traffic offenses, DUI charges, and disorderly conduct. Our team reviews every detail of your case to build a strong defense and explore all possible options.

We are here to provide clear guidance, explain your rights, and help you make informed decisions. With a personalized approach, we ensure your unique circumstances are addressed with care and precision, so you can move forward with confidence.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Municipal Court Matters Explained

What should I do if I get a traffic ticket in New Jersey?

If you receive a traffic ticket in New Jersey, it’s important to consider your options arefully before pleading guilty or paying the fine. Our team can review your case, explain potential consequences, and explore ways to fight the ticket or reduce the penalties.

What are the penalties for a DUI in New Jersey?

Penalties for a DUI in New Jersey can include license suspension, fines, mandatory education programs, and even jail time. We can help you understand the specific penalties for your case and develop a strategy to minimize their impact.

How can I avoid points on my driving record?

Avoiding points on your driving record often requires negotiating your case in court or attending a defensive driving course. We’ll work to reduce or eliminate points through strategic legal representation and personalized advice on next steps.
