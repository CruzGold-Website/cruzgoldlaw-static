Can I file the paperwork myself instead of hiring a lawyer?

While it’s possible to file on your own, the U.S. immigration system is notoriously complex. A single mistake on a form or missing document can lead to long delays or even permanent denials, costing you precious time and money.

Hiring our firm isn’t just about paperwork; it’s an investment in certainty. We ensure your case is prepared perfectly from the start, giving you the strongest possible chance for a smooth approval.

 How much will it cost to bring my family to the U.S.?

We believe in complete transparency. That’s why we offer flat-fee pricing for most family-based cases. After your initial consultation, you’ll receive a clear, upfront cost with no hidden fees or surprise hourly bills. You will know exactly what your investment is before we begin. This allows you to budget for your family’s future with confidence.

How long will the process take?

USCIS processing times can vary widely based on the type of visa and government backlogs. While no firm can guarantee a specific timeline, our 30-year legacy has taught us how to avoid common pitfalls that cause preventable delays. Our priority is to prepare and file a flawless application as quickly as possible, setting your family up for the most efficient journey home.

What if my case is complicated?

Every family’s story is unique, and many cases have complexities. As the son of immigrants and with decades of firm experience, we have handled a vast range of situations. Do not assume your case is too difficult. The first step is to discuss the specifics with us. We will give you an honest assessment and a clear strategy.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes, absolutely. Our team is fluent in Spanish. As a firm founded by immigrants for the immigrant community, we believe you should never have to worry about being understood. We will discuss your case with you in the language you are most comfortable with, from the first call to the final decision, ensuring you can tell your story with confidence and clarity.
