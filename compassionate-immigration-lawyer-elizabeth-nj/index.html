To stand between you and the system.

Navigating the Elizabeth Detention Center and Immigration Court

The Elizabeth Contract Detention Facility (ECDF) sits at 625 Evans Street, tucked away in an industrial park. It is an imposing structure. We understand the specific type of anxiety that hits you the moment you pull up to the building; it’s a feeling many of our clients know all too well.

Finding a place to park is notoriously difficult. Since there is no dedicated lot for visitors, you are forced to rely on street parking. It can be unpredictable. That is why we always advise families to arrive at least thirty minutes early. You really need that buffer. It allows you to hunt for a spot and clear the security checkpoint without the panic of rushing (which only adds to the stress of the day).

Visiting a detained relative requires precision. You cannot simply walk in.

Before you even leave your house, the detainee must add your name to an approved list. When you arrive, make sure you have valid government identification in hand. A critical reminder: leave all electronics in your vehicle. That includes your cell phone. Security protocols are incredibly strict, and they will not let these items through.

Inside the Courtroom

The Elizabeth Immigration Court operates directly inside this facility. Unlike non-detained dockets, things move quickly here.

The bench features experienced adjudicators. These judges preside over removal proceedings with strict adherence to procedural expectations, and they demand preparedness. In our experience, vague answers or missing documentation can severely hurt a case in these specific courtrooms.

A common question we hear is how someone ends up in this position.

The grounds for deportation usually involve overstaying a visa, criminal convictions, or entering the country without inspection. Once those removal proceedings begin, the clock starts ticking.

Our job at Cruz Gold & Associates is to slow that clock down. We need time to build a defense. We navigate these courtrooms daily, so we know exactly what these judges expect from counsel and clients alike. But fighting deportation is only one part of the equation. Often, the best defense is establishing a legal right to stay.

Family-Based Petitions and Humanitarian Visas

The goal often shifts. We want to establish valid residency before a judge even picks up a pen to issue an order.

There are currently over 41 million immigrants in the United States. That is a massive number. A vast majority of these individuals are simply striving to reunite with spouses, children, or parents, and we make these cases a priority at Cruz Gold & Associates. We know the profound pain that comes with separation. Whether the situation calls for filing an I-130 petition for a relative or securing a fiancé visa, our objective stays the same. Keeping your family together right here in New Jersey.

But the path isn’t always linear.

For those who have endured significant hardship, other avenues exist. If you have been a victim of human trafficking or certain crimes, you might qualify for T Visas or U Visas. These humanitarian protections do more than offer legal status. They provide safety. We handle these sensitive matters with deep care (and total confidentiality). To us, you are never just a case number. You are a person deserving of protection.

The process itself depends heavily on where you are currently standing. Some clients can apply for an adjustment of status without ever leaving U.S. soil. Others do not have that luxury. They must navigate consular processing abroad, which can be confusing without the right guidance.

Then there is the financial aspect. It weighs on almost everyone’s mind. You are likely asking, “How much is the actual immigration fee for a green card?” Generally, government filing fees land somewhere between $1,200 and $1,700 (depending on your specific category and biometric requirements), though it is important to remember that these figures can shift. We help you map out every expense well in advance. No surprises. We want to ensure you are never caught off guard by the bottom line.

Family isn’t the only way to build a life here.

Employment-Based Visas: EB-1 and EB-2 Categories

Professional ambition often leads talented individuals right to our doorstep.

While our firm focuses heavily on keeping families together, we know that your career achievements can serve as an equally solid foundation for permanent residence. We partner directly with businesses and driven professionals to secure these high-preference visas. Because these aren’t just temporary work permits. They are pathways to building a lasting, stable future with your family here in the United States.

Prioritizing Extraordinary Ability

The EB-1 category is reserved for those classified as “priority workers.” You might be eligible if you possess extraordinary ability in the sciences, arts, education, business, or athletics. Yet this path extends to others as well. It covers outstanding professors, researchers, and multinational executives operating at the very peak of their fields. The standards here are rigorous.

Proving that you meet these criteria requires substantial evidence. It is a heavy lift. That said, the independence gained is often worth the effort. A major advantage here is that many EB-1 applicants can bypass the need for a specific job offer or labor certification, allowing you to stand firmly on your own merits. 

Advanced Degrees and Exceptional Skills

For professionals holding advanced degrees or those demonstrating exceptional ability, the EB-2 visa offers another viable route.

Unlike the priority worker category, this path typically requires a specific job offer and a labor certification (PERM). But exceptions exist. If your work is deemed vital to the U.S., we can explore a waiver based on the National Interest.

Navigating these petitions is rarely straightforward.

We represent your interests regardless of where your corporate headquarters might be located. We manage the detailed legal strategy. You focus on your professional growth. Securing a visa is a triumph. But sometimes legal complications arise that threaten that hard-won status.

Deportation Defense and Removal Proceedings

Receiving a Notice to Appear (NTA) effectively stops your world cold. It is a terrifying moment.

But an NTA is not a departure order. It is the beginning of a legal battle where the right strategy makes the difference between staying in Elizabeth and being forced to leave. At Cruz Gold & Associates, we take a fiercely protective stance over our clients. We treat your case with the same urgency we would for our own family.

You might ask yourself if hiring a private attorney is truly necessary for this process.

Given the stakes, it is essential. The government will have experienced attorneys working to remove you; you deserve a skilled advocate working just as hard to keep you here. We explore every available avenue of relief. This might mean arguing for cancellation of removal by proving exceptional hardship to your U.S. citizen children or spouse. In other instances, we build robust asylum claims.

Our approach is aggressive in court but compassionate with you. Your life here is worth defending. We won’t give up on your future.

While the fight is necessary, we also understand that practical concerns about expenses are likely weighing on your mind.

Understanding Legal Costs and Resources

Transparency builds trust. Simple as that.

We know that alongside the heavy emotional weight of an immigration case, the financial aspect is a major worry for many families here in Elizabeth. One of the first things clients ask us is exactly how much a U.S. immigration lawyer charges, but the answer isn’t a single flat number. It shifts based entirely on how complex your situation is.

Think about the work involved.

A standard green card renewal involves a very different set of resources than a complicated waiver application or a high-stakes defense against removal. When you invest in private representation, you ensure you get dedicated attention and a strategy tailored specifically to your history rather than a generic approach.

You might be wondering, “Can I get free immigration advice?”

Technically, yes. Non-profit organizations and legal aid clinics perform essential work for individuals who meet specific income qualifications. But we have to be honest about the logistics involved here. These groups are frequently buried under massive caseloads (and often operate with limited staff). If your situation requires immediate, personalized answers rather than a spot on a long waiting list, relying on a pro bono slot might not be a viable option for you.

A warning, though.

Be very cautious about who you ask for free advice. Relying on internet forums or “notarios”, who are not attorneys, can lead to mistakes that are impossible to fix. General information is not the same thing as legal strategy.

At Cruz Gold & Associates, we provide clarity on fees right from the start so you can plan effectively. Your future is an investment. Not a gamble.
