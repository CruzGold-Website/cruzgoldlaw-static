Where your case is heard

Interviews and appointments at USCIS in Philadelphia

Fingerprints and photos (biometrics) for applicants in the city are taken at the Application Support Center, 10300 Drummond Road in Northeast Philadelphia.

A marriage green card case filed by a couple living in the city usually ends with an interview at the USCIS Philadelphia Field Office, and we prepare both spouses for it. The same office handles adjustment of status cases for other relatives and citizenship applications. Petitions for parents and children and K-1 fiancé(e) visas also start with USCIS, though a relative abroad finishes the process at a U.S. consulate.

Asylum applicants in Pennsylvania

USCIS interviews asylum applicants from Philadelphia and most counties of eastern and central Pennsylvania at its Newark Asylum Office, 100 Mulberry Street in Newark, New Jersey. An application filed more than a year after arrival needs an exception, such as changed conditions in your home country, so tell us your arrival date at the first call.

In the Philadelphia Immigration Court

For a client with a case in this court, we choose the relief to apply for (asylum, cancellation of removal or another form), prepare the evidence and testimony, request bond when a detained client qualifies, and appeal to the Board of Immigration Appeals if the judge rules against us.

Our work in Philadelphia

Green cards and visas through work

The EB-2 National Interest Waiver and EB-1A let researchers, physicians and others with a strong record apply for a green card without an employer sponsor. When an employer does sponsor, the usual route is PERM labor certification and EB-3.

Temporary visas we prepare:

  • H-1B for specialty occupations
  • O-1 for extraordinary ability
  • L-1 for transfers within a company
  • TN for Canadian and Mexican professionals
  • E-2 for treaty investors

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free consultation with an immigration lawyer in Philadelphia?

Yes. Your first consultation with Zachary Gold is free.

How much does an immigration lawyer cost in Philadelphia?

We outline the expected costs of your case at the free consultation, and government filing fees are paid on top. Typical ranges by type of case are in our guide to immigration lawyer costs.

Where is the Philadelphia Immigration Court?

900 Market Street, Suite 504, Philadelphia, PA 19107. To see the date of your next hearing, enter the A-number from your notice in the court system's Automated Case Information portal.

Can I go to USCIS in Philadelphia without an appointment?

No. USCIS field offices do not accept walk-ins. To ask for an in-person appointment, call the USCIS Contact Center at 800-375-5283.

How long does citizenship take in Philadelphia?

11.5 months for Form N-400 at the Philadelphia field office, in the USCIS figures of October 19, 2026, that our N-400 processing time guide compares with Newark and Houston.
