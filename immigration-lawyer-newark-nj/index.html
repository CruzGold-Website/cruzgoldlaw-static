Is there a free consultation with an immigration lawyer in Newark?

Yes, your first consultation with attorney Zachary Gold is free. Newark clients meet us by video or at our office at 830 Bear Tavern Road in Ewing, about an hour south by car.

What is the address of USCIS in Newark?

The Newark Field Office is in the Peter W. Rodino Federal Building, 970 Broad Street, Newark, NJ 07102. It sees people by appointment only, so bring the appointment notice with you.

How do I check my case at the Newark Immigration Court?

Call the court system's automated line at 1-800-898-7180 or use its Automated Case Information portal with your A-number. For other questions, the Newark court's phone number is 973-645-3524.

How do I find someone held at Delaney Hall?

Use ICE's Online Detainee Locator. It searches by the nine-digit A-number, or by the exact first and last name, and in both cases you also select the person's country of birth.
