Cruz Gold & Associates, Immigration Lawyers

Your Family, Our Priority

Marta Cruz Gold founded the firm in 1995, and her son, attorney Zachary Gold, leads it today. We handle family, employment and citizenship cases and removal defense for clients across New Jersey and in Philadelphia and Houston, in English or Spanish.

How can we help?

Pick a time below for a free 20-minute consultation with attorney Zachary Gold.

What We Handle

Family & Citizenship

Petitions for spouses, parents, children and siblings, fiancé(e) visas, and green cards through adjustment of status. When you qualify, we also prepare your citizenship application (Form N-400) and get you ready for the interview.

  • Family-Based Immigration
  • Marriage Green Card
  • K-1 Fiancé(e) Visa
  • Adjustment of Status
  • Citizenship & Naturalization

Employment Green Cards

Permanent residence through your work, a job offer or an investment. We handle self-petitions such as EB-1A and the National Interest Waiver as well as employer-sponsored PERM cases.

  • EB-1A Extraordinary Ability
  • EB-2 National Interest Waiver
  • EB-3 and PERM
  • EB-5 Investor

Work & Student Visas

Temporary visas for professionals, company transferees, investors and students. We prepare the petition, answer requests for evidence and plan the extension or the next status.

  • All Work Visas
  • H-1B
  • O-1
  • L-1
  • E-2 Investor
  • TN (Canada and Mexico)
  • Student Visas

Removal Defense

If you or a family member has received a Notice to Appear, we can represent the person named on it in immigration court. Call us as soon as it arrives, and have the notice in front of you when we talk.

  • Talk to an attorney
  • Fees for court cases

  1. Free 20-minute consultationBook a time online with attorney Zachary Gold and tell him what happened. He looks for roadblocks before anything is filed.
  2. Your fee, before any work beginsGreen card, citizenship and family petitions usually have a flat fee; court cases are billed by the hour. Government filing fees are separate, and we accept credit cards.

Remembering Marta Cruz Gold (1969-2023)

Founder of Cruz Gold & Associates (Law Office of Marta Cruz Gold)

We honor the memory of Marta Cruz Gold, our founder and a beacon in the legal community. Marta’s legal journey began at Rutgers University School of Law, leading to the establishment of her practice, The Law Office of Marta Cruz Gold, in 1995. Known for her dedication to justice and mentorship, she left a lasting impact both in and out of the courtroom.

Marta’s legacy of integrity, excellence, and empathy continues to guide us. Her commitment and passion to the realm of immigration law and her role as a community leader in Mercer County remain inspirations to us all.

Marta passed away on June 12, 2023, but her spirit lives on in the work we do and the lives she touched. Cruz Gold & Associates is dedicated to upholding Marta’s values and continuing her mission to provide compassionate legal services in Ewing, NJ.

Common Questions

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first 20-minute consultation with attorney Zachary Gold is free, and you can book it online.

How much will my case cost?

It depends on the case. We explain the fee before any work begins, and our fees page lists typical ranges for each kind of case.

How long does an N-400 take at the Newark USCIS office?

As of August 18, 2026, USCIS estimated 14.5 months for an N-400 at the Newark field office. Our N-400 processing time guide has the Philadelphia and Houston numbers too.
