Petitions for spouses, parents, children and siblings, fiancé(e) visas, and green cards through adjustment of status. When you qualify, we also prepare your citizenship application (Form N-400) and get you ready for the interview.

Employment Green Cards

Permanent residence through your work, a job offer or an investment. We handle self-petitions such as EB-1A and the National Interest Waiver as well as employer-sponsored PERM cases.

Common Questions

How much does an immigration lawyer cost?

Most green card, citizenship and family petitions have a flat fee, court cases are billed by the hour, and government filing fees are separate. You hear our fee before any work begins, and our guide to immigration lawyer costs lists typical ranges.

Do immigration lawyers offer free consultations?

Many do, and we do. The first 20-minute consultation with attorney Zachary Gold is free, and you can book it online.

Do I need an immigration lawyer for a green card?

The law does not require one. A lawyer matters most when your history has a complication: an earlier overstay or entry without inspection, an arrest or conviction, a past denial, or a case already in immigration court. In those cases a filing mistake can lead to a denial or to removal proceedings.

What does an immigration lawyer do?

An immigration lawyer prepares and files applications with USCIS, gets clients ready for their interviews, and represents them in immigration court. Our cases cover family petitions, green cards through adjustment of status, citizenship, work visas and removal defense.

Can an immigration lawyer speed up my case?

A lawyer cannot change how fast USCIS works through its queue. USCIS grants an expedite request only in limited situations, such as severe financial loss or an urgent humanitarian reason, and some work petitions can use premium processing for an extra fee. A complete filing lowers the chance of a request for evidence, which can add months to a case.
